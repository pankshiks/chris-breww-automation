Section 1

What You Are Building

A B2B-only trade store on Shopify Grow plan for Podkovan UK, running at trade.podkovan.com. This is not a consumer store. It is exclusively for pre-approved trade customers who have already been onboarded through the Podkovan trade account application process. It must look and work as one site with the new brand site, podkovan.com.

How it fits into the wider system

Your Shopify store is one piece of a larger integration. Here is where it sits:

Customer journey: Trade application form → n8n approval → Breww customer created → Shopify login invitation sent → Customer orders on Shopify → Order lands in Breww → n8n raises Xero invoice → n8n sends depot notification.

Your job: Build and configure the Shopify store correctly. The n8n developer handles everything that happens after the order lands in Breww.

Section 2

The Rules

These are non-negotiable. Break any of them and the wider integration will fail.

Must NOT
Connect the Shopify Xero app
n8n creates all Xero invoices via API. A second Xero connection will cause duplicate invoices.
Must NOT
Allow customer self-registration
Every trade customer must be approved in Breww first. No unapproved customer should ever reach the store.
Must NOT
Enable Shopify invoice emails
Xero sends all VAT invoices. Shopify sending its own invoice emails will cause duplicate communication to customers.
Must NOT
Enable Shopify payment reminders
Xero handles all payment reminders. Shopify reminders would duplicate this.
Must NOT
Allow Shopify to create Breww customers
Breww is the single source of truth for customers. Only the onboarding workflow creates Breww customers. Validate that this setting is off in the Breww native integration.
Must
Enable Shopify order confirmation emails
This is the only Shopify email that should send. Customers expect to receive it after placing an order.
Must
Keep Shopify Grow plan — max 3 price catalogues
Customer-specific pricing requires Shopify Plus. If Chris needs it, raise it before B2 starts.
Must NOT
Send customers to a third-party store
Main menu items (Trade shop, Our beer, About, Find Podkovan, Contact) may only lead to podkovan.com or trade.podkovan.com. Never to a retailer's store.
Must NOT
Use any brand spec other than the current brand brief
The brand brief from Chris replaces any earlier spec. Ignore older colours, fonts or layouts.
Must NOT
Add custom theme development
Use the simplest free theme, theme settings and light CSS only. Where the theme has limits, podkovan.com adapts to the store rather than adding custom code.
Must
Match podkovan.com as one site
Same logo, Joane Engraved headings, Podkovan gold #FFA500, header, menu, announcement bar and footer, as set out in the brand brief.
Must
Always give a way back to podkovan.com
The store logo and the content menu items link to the matching pages on podkovan.com.
Must
Keep checkout, accounts and order data in our own Shopify store
No third-party storefronts, and no data passed to another business.

Shopify Shipping Confirmation emails — confirm with Chris before B2. This is an open question. Do not make an assumption. If Chris hasn't answered, leave disabled and document it as a placeholder.
